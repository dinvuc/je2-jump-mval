New in the culture

  • Instant retry. At n = 0 the cell bursts and the part restarts by itself. The results count your lost replicates (☠).
  • Split times. The PART 2 gate shows your part-1 time: green if you are ahead of your best run, red if behind.
  • Share. On results screens, T copies a one-line result, L copies a link that lets a friend race your ghost, I saves a micrograph screenshot (also in play).
  • Daily challenge. One part, one strain, one rule of the day, the same for everyone.
  • Log phase. A generated climb of 20 generations while a rising front chases you. A new climb every run.
  • Codex (B on the title, or the pause menu). Everything you met, with the checked biology notes and a peptide album.
  • Secrets and chains. One hidden room per map (look for cracked walls). Stomp enemies without landing for DOUBLE! TRIPLE!
  • Replays. When a part took several attempts, all of them run at once on a map of the part.
  • Calmer tips. Info signs speak only after you stand on the i for a second, in a small see-through panel.
  • Animations. Faces that react, eyes that follow, a double-jump spin, bouncing blocks, LasA wind-ups, pickups flying into the HUD, stamped results.
