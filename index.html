New in the culture

  • Jumps carry. The cell keeps its speed in the air, hangs a moment at the top of a held jump and falls a little faster. Still three rows high.
  • Smooth on fast screens. Motion stays even on 90, 120 and 144 Hz displays.
  • A calmer screen. The goal list folds into a small pill and reopens when a goal changes; reminders fade after 8 s; messages move away from your cell.
  • Fixed. The SNpulse deadline goal is no longer ticked from the start.
  • Quicker start. A first visit starts TSB straight away; the full menu opens after the first level.
  • Phones. Hold it sideways; upright it pauses and asks you to turn it. Bigger buttons and text.
  • Reduce motion in the pause menu: no screen shake, flashes or blinking.
  • Music. A new B section alternates with the theme.
  • Playtesters: open the game with ?debug=deaths for a heat map of where you got hit; J copies it.
